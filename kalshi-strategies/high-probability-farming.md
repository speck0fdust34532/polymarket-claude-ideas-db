# High-Probability Farming: Kalshi Trading Bot (2026)

## Overview
**High-Probability Farming** is a low-risk, consistent-yield strategy designed specifically for Kalshi. It focuses on identifying and "farming" markets with a very high probability of a specific outcome, often in the macro or political space.

## The Strategy
The core of the strategy is to identify markets where the consensus probability is extremely high (e.g., >95%) and provide liquidity or take positions that yield a small but reliable profit. While the returns per trade are low, the high win rate and compounding effect can lead to significant monthly gains.

## Claude-Assisted Selection
Claude is used to perform deep-dive analysis on the "tail risks" of these high-probability markets. By using a specialized Claude skill for macro research, the bot can:
- **Analyze Edge Cases:** Identify scenarios where the 95% probability might be mispriced (e.g., an unexpected Fed move).
- **Sentiment Monitoring:** Track X and Reddit for any breaking news that could disrupt the "sure thing."
- **Risk Management:** Adjust position sizes based on Claude's confidence score for the high-probability outcome.

## Implementation Details
- **Platform:** Kalshi (due to its regulated nature and specific market types).
- **Execution:** Automated via the Kalshi API.
- **Selection:** High-probability (>95%) macro or political events.
- **Edge:** Claude-driven tail-risk analysis.

## Performance Metrics
Traders using this strategy in early 2026 have reported consistent monthly returns of **3-5%** with very low drawdowns. The key is to avoid "black swan" events by using Claude's superior reasoning to monitor for potential shifts in the underlying thesis.

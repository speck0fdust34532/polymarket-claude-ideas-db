# NOAA/NWS Data Integration for Weather Trading

- **Concept:** Integrating real-time weather data from NOAA and NWS to predict outcomes in Polymarket weather markets.
- **How Claude Assists:** Claude can process raw NOAA/NWS forecast data, compare it against Polymarket market prices (e.g., temperature buckets, precipitation probabilities), and identify discrepancies for arbitrage opportunities.
- **Data Sources:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), National Weather Service (NWS).
- **Transaction Volume:** HIGH (requires frequent data pulls and rapid trading to exploit short-lived edges).
- **Durability:** MEDIUM (edge can be crowded as more automated bots enter, but continuous updates in weather data provide persistent opportunities).

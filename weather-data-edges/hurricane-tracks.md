# Hurricane Track Analysis for Weather Trading

- **Concept:** Trading Polymarket markets related to hurricane landfalls, intensity, or specific geographic impacts by analyzing real-time hurricane track data.
- **How Claude Assists:** Claude can process complex meteorological models and forecast updates (e.g., from NOAA's National Hurricane Center), synthesize information about potential market impacts, and identify mispricings in Polymarket contracts related to hurricane events.
- **Data Sources:** NOAA National Hurricane Center (NHC) advisories, weather models (GFS, ECMWF).
- **Transaction Volume:** LOW (Event-driven, specific to hurricane season and active storms).
- **Durability:** HIGH (Specialized knowledge and rapid data processing provide a strong edge; events are less frequent but high impact).

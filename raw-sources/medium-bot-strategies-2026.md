## Core Strategies
1. **Automated Market Making (AMM):**
   - **Mechanism:** Providing liquidity to both sides of a market and earning the spread.
   - **Operation:** Bots monitor orderbook depth, adjusting orders every 30 seconds, and managing inventory.
   - **Expected Returns:** 1–3% monthly.

2. **AI-Powered Probability Arbitrage:**
   - **Mechanism:** Using ensemble AI models to analyze news and social signals rapidly.
   - **Operation:** Identifying when market prices lag behind new information and executing trades within seconds to minutes.
   - **Expected Returns:** 3–8% monthly.

3. **Correlation and Logical Arbitrage:**
   - **Mechanism:** Exploiting pricing inconsistencies between correlated markets.
   - **Operation:** Executing multi-leg trades where probabilities violate logical relationships (e.g., if Team A wins, Conference B must win).
   - **Win Rate:** 70–80%.

4. **High-Frequency Momentum Trading:**
   - **Mechanism:** Detecting early trend signals from news, orderbook surges, and whale activity.
   - **Operation:** Rapid trades to ride momentum before reversals.
   - **Expected Returns:** 8–15% monthly (high volatility).

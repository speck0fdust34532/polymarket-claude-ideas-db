# Claude as a Trading Second Brain

- **Concept:** Leveraging Claude AI as an intelligent assistant for comprehensive market analysis, thesis generation, and trade idea formulation across stocks, options, and futures.
- **How Claude Assists:**
    - **Research & Thesis Generation:** Claude scans live market data (e.g., Polygon.io), earnings reports, SEC filings, and options flow (IV/HV, unusual volume) to identify opportunities and generate trading theses.
    - **Trade Idea Formulation:** Creates "trade cards" with entry/exit points, stop-loss, and profit targets.
    - **Automated Journaling:** Logs trades with rationale to Google Sheets or Notion.
- **Transaction Volume:** LOW to MEDIUM (focused on high-conviction swing trades or credit spreads).
- **Tax Complexity:** LOW to MEDIUM (can be managed by holding trades >30 days to avoid wash sales; futures may qualify for 60/40 tax treatment under Section 1256).
- **Durability:** HIGH (personalized alpha, adaptable to market changes, focuses on quality over quantity).
- **Real Example:** A Reddit user (Plastic-Edge-1654) developed a system scanning 500 stocks daily for trade suggestions with plain English explanations.
- **Key Tools:** MCP (Model Context Protocol), Alpaca API, TradeStation API, custom Python scripts.

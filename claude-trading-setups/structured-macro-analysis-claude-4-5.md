# Structured Macro Analysis with Claude 4.5

## Strategy Overview
Using Claude 4.5 Sonnet's superior reasoning and context window to break down complex macro events (like CPI, NFP, Fed decisions) into sub-components for better probability estimation.

## How it Works
1.  **Component Breakdown**: Instead of asking for a single probability, ask Claude to analyze the components of a macro event.
    *   **Example for CPI**:
        1.  **Shelter component** (40% of CPI): Trending down but sticky.
        2.  **Energy prices**: Oil prices, energy demand, and seasonal factors.
        3.  **Base effects**: Comparison to the same month in the previous year.
        4.  **Fed commentary**: Language from recent speeches and meetings.
2.  **Probability Range**: Ask for a base-case probability and a worst-case probability based on specific uncertainties.
3.  **Bayesian Updating**: Use Claude to update probabilities as new data points (e.g., intermediate oil prices or retail sales) come in.

## Why it Works in 2026
*   **Deep Reasoning**: Claude 4.5 Sonnet's improved reasoning capabilities allow it to handle complex, multi-factor analyses that generic AIs often struggle with.
*   **Large Context Window**: Can ingest and process long reports, speeches, and datasets to provide a more informed analysis.

## Key Advantages
*   **Systematic Approach**: Reduces reliance on "gut feeling" and provides a more auditable reasoning process.
*   **Uncertainty Quantification**: Explicitly identifies and quantifies the impact of key uncertainties.

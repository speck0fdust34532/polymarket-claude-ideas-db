# Prediction Market Hype Trading Strategy

A unique approach to stock market trading detailed by Jonathan Rose of InvestorPlace, which uses Polymarket and Kalshi prediction data to front-run traditional equity earnings moves. [1]

## Core Philosophy

The fundamental premise of this strategy is that prediction markets (Polymarket, Kalshi) price in shifting probabilities faster than Wall Street analysts revise their estimates, and faster than stock prices fully adjust.

Instead of focusing on headlines, revenue beats, or EPS misses, this strategy asks: "What is already priced in?"

## The Strategy Mechanics

When millions of dollars are wagered on a specific real-world event (e.g., government subsidies, tariff rulings, geopolitical outcomes), the belief of the crowd adjusts quickly. The strategy exploits the temporary misalignment between these rapidly shifting prediction market probabilities and the slower-moving equity markets.

### The "Gap"

Opportunities appear when belief changes but stocks haven't caught up yet.

If probability markets suddenly reprice the odds of tariffs, subsidies, policy shifts, or macro outcomes, and equities are still trading on outdated assumptions, a temporary pricing gap forms. Markets do not tolerate this misalignment for long.

### Execution

1.  **Monitor High-Volume Prediction Markets:** Track contracts with significant capital (thousands to millions) wagered on specific outcomes, such as government investments in specific sectors.
2.  **Identify Correlated Equities:** Find the specific stocks tied to those outcomes (e.g., semiconductor manufacturers, suppliers of key materials).
3.  **Cross-Reference Institutional Signals:** Combine the prediction market probability data with key institutional signals showing where smart money is positioning *before* earnings prints hit the tape.
4.  **Trade the Misalignment:** Enter structured trades (often options) on the correlated equities before the broader market reprices based on the event outcome or the upcoming earnings report.

## Case Studies and Results

The strategy claims significant success by identifying rapid moves in stocks during earnings season and beyond:

*   **Rigetti Computing (RGTI):** 233% gain in five days.
*   **TMC:** 700%+ gain in just over two months, driven by government-led volatility, a strong earnings beat, and early probability signals from prediction markets.
*   **Sunrun Inc. (RUN):** 151% in two days when tariff confidence deteriorated late in the year.
*   **BHP Group Ltd. (BHP):** 189% in 17 days.
*   **Alphatec Holdings Inc. (ATEC):** 213% return on a structured trade, while the stock itself moved just 21% before earnings.
*   **Fastly Inc. (FSLY):** 300%+ in just over a month.
*   **Snap Inc. (SNAP):** 375%+ combined in about two months, despite the stock dropping 44% after earnings.

During one specific quarter, the strategy reported a 60% win rate with an average return of 85.76% over roughly 31 days.

## Why This Edge Exists

Prediction markets do not replace fundamentals; they enhance them. They provide a stronger sense of the odds market participants are pricing in and where they are positioning ahead of big moves. Hype, in this context, is treated as a quantifiable signal that smart traders use to stay ahead of the crowd, rather than noise to be ignored.

# Multi-Pillar Market Analysis

## Strategy Overview
The Multi-Pillar Analysis strategy uses a multi-framework analytical engine that runs independent expert models (Pillars) to analyze different aspects of a specific market simultaneously. This approach provides a comprehensive, evidence-based analysis for prediction markets.

## How it Works
1. **Pillar Definition:** Define specialized analytical frameworks (Pillars) for different types of markets.
   - **Example: Fed Rate Decision Pillars:**
     - Fed Communication Pillar (parses FOMC minutes, Powell speeches)
     - CPI Trend Analysis Pillar (analyzes inflation components)
     - Employment Strength Pillar (NFP, jobless claims, JOLTS)
     - Fed Funds Futures Pillar (compares Kalshi to CME pricing)
     - Historical Pattern Pillar
     - Market Positioning Pillar (is Kalshi mispriced vs fundamentals?)
2. **Independent Analysis:** Each pillar operates independently, analyzing its specific data source and generating a probability and confidence score.
3. **Synthesis:** All pillar results are synthesized into a single verdict with a quantified edge.
4. **Execution:** Execute trades based on the synthesized probability and the quantified edge.

## Key Advantages
- **Comprehensive Analysis:** Covers multiple facets of a market, reducing the risk of missing critical information.
- **Evidence-Based:** Relies on specific, quantifiable data points rather than general model knowledge.
- **Confidence Scoring:** Provides a quantified confidence level for each individual pillar and the overall verdict.

# Ultra-Short Crypto Bets on Polymarket & Kalshi

## Strategy Overview
Ultra-short crypto bets, particularly 5-minute Bitcoin and Ethereum markets, have become a dominant flow on prediction platforms in 2026. This strategy leverages high-frequency data and AI-driven analysis to exploit short-term price movements and volatility.

## How it Works
1. **Data Feeding:** Traders feed weeks of historical price data (e.g., Bitcoin 1-minute candles) into advanced AI models like Claude 4.5 or 4.6.
2. **Short-Term Prediction:** The AI identifies micro-patterns and sentiment shifts to predict the outcome of 5-minute or 15-minute price intervals.
3. **Execution:** Automated bots execute trades based on the AI's probability estimates, often competing with HFT firms for the best entry prices.
4. **Volume Management:** High daily volumes (up to $70M) provide significant liquidity for these ultra-short markets.

## Key Advantages
- **High Turnover:** Multiple trading opportunities per hour allow for rapid compounding of gains.
- **AI Edge:** Leveraging LLMs for pattern recognition in noisy short-term data.
- **Market Dominance:** These markets now drive a significant portion of total platform volume.
